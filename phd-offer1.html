PhD Thesis Offer

Collective Representations in Multi-Agent Systems: Emergent Properties and Shared Mental Models

Project Description

This PhD project investigates the emergence of collective representations in artificial multi-agent systems. Drawing from both psychological theories of collective consciousness and contemporary AI architectures, the project aims to develop novel frameworks for understanding how artificial agents can share and build collective mental models.

The central research questions include:

  • How can we model the emergence of shared representations across distributed AI agents?
  • What mechanisms facilitate the development of collective intelligence beyond the sum of individual agent capabilities?
  • How might Jungian concepts of collective unconscious inspire new computational models for shared knowledge representation?
  • What novel architectures can support the formation and maintenance of collective artificial consciousness?

The successful candidate will work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, cognitive science, and philosophy of mind, developing experimental frameworks to test hypotheses about collective agent representations.

Requirements for PhD Candidate

  • Master's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cognitive Science, or related field
  • Strong programming skills, particularly in Python and frameworks for AI/ML (PyTorch, TensorFlow)
  • Background knowledge in multi-agent systems, distributed AI, or collective intelligence
  • Interest in interdisciplinary research bridging AI and cognitive science
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills in English
  • Experience with large language models and foundation models (desirable)
  • Background in philosophy of mind or cognitive psychology (desirable)
